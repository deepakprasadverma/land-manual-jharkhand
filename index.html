About the Author

Salendra Kumar Verma

Foremost Authority on Jharkhand Land Revenue Laws, Tenancy Acts & Property Litigation

Salendra Kumar Verma is a veteran legal practitioner, consultant, and trusted authority on Jharkhand’s intricate land tenure jurisprudence. Over three decades of courtroom advocacy and administrative practice, he has resolved hundreds of high-stakes land title conflicts, contested mutations, and tribal tenancy disputes across Ranchi, Dhanbad, Jamshedpur, Bokaro, and the Santhal Pargana division.

His mission with "Land Manual of Jharkhand" is to distill dense statutory jargon into actionable, transparent guidance—protecting common citizens from property traps while giving advocates, corporate developers, and revenue officers an authoritative reference standard.
