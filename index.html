What This Manual Covers

Every crucial aspect of Jharkhand real estate law structured into four practical pillars.

Land Laws & Procedures

Deep analysis of the Chota Nagpur Tenancy (CNT) Act and Santhal Parganas Tenancy (SPT) Act, land classifications (Bakast, Gair Mazarua Aam/Khas), and statutory transfer restrictions.

Property Buying & Selling Guidelines

A step-by-step checklist to avoid fraudulent deals: title verification, encumbrance verification, agreement drafting, and safely navigating power-of-attorney transactions.

Documents & Registration Process

Clear walkthrough of Khatian, Jamabandi, Register-II verification, stamp duty calculations, online portal procedures (Jharbhoomi), and official Dakhil-Kharij (Mutation).

Legal Clarity & Practical Solutions

Actionable remedies for disputed mutations, boundary disputes, ancestral partition complications, and guidance on approaching Land Revenue Courts and appellate authorities.

About the Author

Salendra Kumar Verma

Distinguished Revenue Law Authority, Practitioner & Land Reforms Consultant

Salendra Kumar Verma is an acclaimed legal scholar and practitioner with decades of specialized experience in Jharkhand's land revenue administration and property jurisprudence. Having observed firsthand the complexities and disputes arising from unverified transactions, CNT/SPT ambiguity, and mismanaged land records, he authored this manual to bridge the gap between statutory law and practical ground realities.

His guidance has assisted legal practitioners, revenue officials, real estate consultants, and common citizens in safeguarding their land investments and navigating complex bureaucratic frameworks with confidence.
